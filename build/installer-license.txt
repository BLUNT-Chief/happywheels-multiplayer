Happy Wheels Multiplayer - unofficial fan-made mod

Race your friends in Happy Wheels: create or join a lobby, the host picks a level,
and everyone starts on the same countdown.

Before you install:

 * You need your own copy of Happy Wheels on Steam. This mod runs that copy; it does
   not include or change any of the game's files.
 * Steam must be running and logged in to use multiplayer lobbies.
 * Start the game from the "Happy Wheels Multiplayer" shortcut. The launcher can also
   set up Happy Wheels in your Steam library to open Multiplayer when you press Play
   (it changes the game's Steam Launch Options; uninstalling puts them back).
 * The mod updates itself automatically.
